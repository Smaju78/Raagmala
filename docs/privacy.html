Privacy

Raagmala is a free, personal, non-commercial website about Hindustani classical vocal music. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not collect personal information.

What is stored, and where

Your likes, the performances you chose never to play, your jukebox filters and a short list of recently played performances are saved in your own browser (local storage). They never leave your device, and you can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Nothing is stored on a server.

Deleting your data

To delete everything, use the browser setting that clears site data for smaju78.github.io. The YouTube data this site keeps (video IDs, titles, channel names and view and like counts) is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days; videos that are removed from YouTube are dropped at the next refresh.

YouTube

Performances are played with the embedded YouTube player, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). When you play a video, YouTube and Google receive information as described in the Google Privacy Policy, and your use of the player is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. This site uses YouTube API Services to find performances; that lookup runs offline, not in your browser.

Other services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and video thumbnails from YouTube's image servers (i.ytimg.com). The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Raag and artist data

Raag and artist facts (names, thaat, time, notes, dates, gharana) are collected from Wikidata and Wikipedia and checked by hand. Stored video details (titles, channels, view and like counts) are refreshed at least every 30 days.
