রাগমালা · design conceptsThree music-app directions for the same content — pick one, or mix.

Each mockup is one self-contained page with real data from ../raagmala.json. Screens: Home (শুনুন) with a “Now” hero for the current prahar, expanded Now Playing / jukebox with filters, raag page, artist page, search, and a Mine stub — plus a persistent mini-player on every screen. Resize the window or open on a phone: every concept has a phone layout (bottom tab bar) and a desktop layout. Nothing actually plays; the YouTube area is a placeholder.

Concept ANocturne

A · Nocturne dark, dense, Spotify-like

A neutral near-black interface with an amber accent and Hind Siliguri throughout. Home is a stack of tight horizontal rows of square tiles; raag tiles are typographic (the full name in bold type with a thaat-coloured band, a prahar glyph and a faint initial as watermark); on desktop the hero carries a collage of the hour's performance thumbnails. Desktop uses a left sidebar with text navigation and a full-width bottom player; phone uses a floating mini-player above the tab bar. Jukebox filters are per-key chips that open a bottom sheet. The most familiar, information-dense option.

Concept Bপ্রহর · Prahar

B · Prahar light, warm, the palette follows the hour

Warm paper with Tiro Bangla serif display type. The whole page is themed by the current prahar: the hero is that hour's sky (sun or moon on a dotted arc), and the accent colour, page tint and the mini-player ribbon shift with it (a row of prahar chips under the hero lets you preview any hour; raag pages take on their own raag's hour). Raag tiles are little skies: the sun or moon where the hour puts it, a thaat-coloured haze on the horizon and a row of twelve dots showing which swaras the raag uses. Desktop uses a top bar with text tabs and a search field; facts are set as a ledger. Supports dark mode via the system preference.

Concept Cচিত্র · Chitra

C · Chitra artwork-led, ragamala miniatures

A deep lacquer base (or parchment, via the ☾ toggle) with gold rules and ornament, Noto Serif Bengali display type. Every raag gets a generated “painting”: a 3:4 framed card in its thaat's ragamala palette with a swara mandala — the twelve notes on a wheel, the raag's notes joined into a constellation, vadi as a gold dot and samvadi as a ring — so each raag is visually unique. Artists sit in arched frames. The hour's raags are a gallery of large cards on Home; desktop uses a narrow icon rail; the jukebox has a single sheet with all seven filter groups at once. The most distinctive identity.

Notes for the final build. All three share the same data helpers, notation renderer (romanised with komal underlined and tivra Ma marked; Bengali akarmatrik with octave dots), filter logic and player state, so the chosen look can be combined with any other concept's layout ideas. Google sign-in, YouTube embed and persistence are stubs. Elements that must survive in the real site: the visible YouTube embed in Now Playing (≥200×200, never under the mini-player), Bengali digits in Bengali mode, keyboard focus rings, and reduced-motion support.
